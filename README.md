# Welcome to the DogFights repository!
Hi everyone! My name is Tom, and this is my game passion project that I work on in my free time. It is called DogFights, and it is about cute dogs fighting each other with (less cute) weapons. Interesting combination, right?

Here is an impression of the start of the game, when all the Pips (a Pip how the white dogs are called) are wavering out from their start-spawn location:

![Start of game impression](images_and_screenshots/start_game_with_pips.png)

In the current state of the game, the Pips and the Black Bears fight each other:

![Pips vs Black Bears](images_and_screenshots/pips_vs_bears.png)

The idea is that each team has a number of forces (reserves), and has to deplete the other team of their forces.

## Robotics algorithms

I also like to apply complex robotics algorithms to my game. An example of this, is the RTT*-inspired motion planning that the computer players use:

![RRT branches](images_and_screenshots/rrt_branches_around_corner.png)

In the future, I would like to implement swarming algorithms, such that the computer players take into account their team players, and keep bonds with them 'warm'. In this way, the computer players will truly show team-based behaviour, and act as a unified 'super-organism'.
